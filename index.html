Our model

Sourcing should start with clarity.

A simple buyer workflow, with evidence requests designed into the process from day one.

01

Define a real need

Tell us the use case, dimensions, desired material, expected quantities, shipping destination, and sourcing timeline.

02

Choose the evidence you need

Specify relevant documents such as chain-of-custody, recycled-content evidence, composition reports, or compostability tests, where applicable.

03

Prepare a supplier-ready RFQ

Generate a structured message you can download, copy, or send through your email app. Buyer submissions are not stored on this website.

04

Compare actual supplier evidence

When quotes and product documentation are available, evaluate quality, cost, lead time, claims, and logistics side by side.

Documentation checklist

What evidence is appropriate depends on the material and market. A starting checklist may include:

  • Material composition and specification sheets
  • Available test reports and supporting certificates
  • Traceability or chain-of-custody where relevant
  • Minimum order quantities and lead times
  • Packaging performance and local disposal conditions
A certificate is not automatically proof that every finished product or marketing claim meets a standard. Check scope, issuer, validity, and applicability.

Frequently asked

Before you begin.

Can I purchase materials on EcoGlobal today?

Not through an online checkout yet. This first release provides category discovery and a structured sourcing enquiry workflow. Supplier listings, quotation management, and transactions will be introduced as the network develops.

Are the materials or suppliers verified?

No individual suppliers are listed or certified by EcoGlobal in this release. We outline evidence to request and evaluate; supplier verification requires checking documentation and individual offers.

Is EcoGlobal only for buyers in India?

No. The platform is designed with international B2B sourcing in mind. Actual supply routes and regulatory requirements vary by country.

How do RFQ submissions work?

The form prepares an email in your default mail app or lets you download/copy your request. It does not silently submit your details to a backend or guarantee that an email has been delivered.

The idea behind EcoGlobal

Better commerce begins
with better questions.

EcoGlobal is an emerging B2B initiative exploring how global sustainable sourcing can become more accessible, evidence-driven and efficient.

Why this exists

Sustainability and practicality belong together.

Businesses making material decisions need more than eco buzzwords. They need suitable products, pricing clarity, quality, technical documents, realistic logistics, and reliable supply relationships. We are creating a platform concept that brings those elements into one structured journey.

Our initial focus is packaging and adjacent material categories. Over time, the platform may expand into additional product families and technology-enabled sourcing workflows as real supply and customer demand are established.
